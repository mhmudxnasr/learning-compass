Route a basic intro for an already-used tool to Inbox/RSS; reject corporate PR; allow a practical applied tool, major model release, or AI hardware analysis when it fits; reject low-level training-math theory. The Worker owns validation, deduplication, scoring, abstention, and receipts. Normal requests return one fit pick and wait for explicit Start. Books require `allow_books: true`. Bridge/challenge require explicit exploration.
- Islamic recommendations must be original lectures/khutbahs/talks by trusted Sunni scholars; no book-derived material. Respect all durable exclusions in `AGENTS.md` and the live profile.

- No scheduled learning-core poller, autonomous recommendation, routine self-improvement message, or background media generation.

No evidence means no change and no database churn. Destructive deletion and external publication remain explicit-only.
